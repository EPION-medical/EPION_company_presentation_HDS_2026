Home Delivery Service

Direct access to the patient –
straight to their door

Alongside institutional distribution, EPION runs a dedicated Home Delivery Service: a wide range of medical aids in one place, delivered to patients' homes on the day that suits them. It is a proven way to reach the end patient.

How it works

A proven process, from first
contact to the doorstep

01RegistrationThe patient registers with EPION by phone, e-mail or through our website.
02Contact & detailsWe contact the patient and collect the additional information needed to supply the right aids.
03Agreed scheduleThe patient's aids are recorded and every shipment is confirmed by phone, so timing always fits.
04Home deliverySupplies arrive discreetly packed at the patient's home – regularly, with no need to reorder.

A repeatable, scheduled routine that keeps patients supplied without the need to reorder.
